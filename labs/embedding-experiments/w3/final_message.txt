# w3: static vs transformer vs LLM embedders

Harness defaults; 2 threads on a shared Mac, so speeds are indicative. SAFE = static or non-neural, no transformer at runtime (* = label-free TF-IDF reference fitted on all 1,897 texts: LSA-300 and RP-512); GREY = multilingual encoders; RISKY = Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (fp32, batch 16; "+instr" = retrieval instruction on rows, "plain" = none) and nomic-v2-moe. Prefixes: rows e5 "query: ", nomic "search_query: "; descriptions e5 "query: ", nomic "search_document: " (alternative logged as `desc=alt_prefix`). Hybrid = z(probe log-prob) + z(zero-shot cosine) over the gold subject's attributes, probe-unseen candidates z=0. "Centered" = mean vector removed first (extra). Tables are sorted by their first metric: rows 1-3 are the top-3 per task. Fold noise is about 0.01.

**Static vs transformers.** Close. The best transformer or LLM leads the best neural static model by at most 0.020 on any headline metric, raw or centered (centered zero-shot: nomic 0.851 vs potion-32M 0.832). Raw, the lead is under 0.01 on hybrid (0.919 vs 0.913), recall@10 and AUC, while static leads probe (0.868 vs 0.859), kNN, zero-shot (0.841 vs Qwen3 0.831; nomic 0.849 with a query-side description prefix), MRR and arena. Versus the TF-IDF+SVM probe (0.873), static-sim-multi hybrid 0.913 and potion-8M 0.902 win, and the 76 unseen rows go from 0.000 to 0.50-0.66. The gain is zero-shot fusion: no embedding probe beats TF-IDF+SVM on 1-4-example attributes (best 0.866 raw, 0.870 centered).

**LLM ceiling.** Not meaningfully higher. Both Qwen3 variants trail the best static model on probe (+instr 0.839 vs 0.868), zero-shot (0.802 vs 0.841), hybrid (0.896 vs 0.913) and AUC (0.577 vs 0.608); on recall@10 Qwen3+instr edges static-sim-multi (0.662 vs 0.653) but not LSA-300 (0.702). Its instruction is mixed: kNN 0.817 vs 0.791 and recall 0.662 vs 0.620 gain, zero-shot 0.802 vs 0.831 loses, probe and hybrid tie, speed drops 20% (~4,500 s per 20k texts on x86). Best RISKY is nomic: hybrid 0.919 raw, 0.933 centered vs 0.913, 0.918 static, at 46.0 sents/s vs 29k-60k and 1.9 GB vs 31-485 MB.

**Surprises.**
1. Anisotropy: centering lifts the e5-small probe 0.722 to 0.864 and Qwen3+instr 0.839 to 0.873; the top six centered probes sit at 0.864-0.873, so raw encoder rankings partly measure geometry. Not an iteration cap (lbfgs stops after 32-60 of 3,000); cause not isolated.
2. The 1:1 hybrid hurts unseen attributes: leave-attribute-out hybrid 0.443-0.622 is below zero-shot alone 0.686-0.841 on all 13 rows, and probe weight 0 is best there (potion-8M 0.835 at weight 0, 0.530 at 1, 0.157 at 2). On page folds weight 1 is best for 10 of 13 (others at most 0.020 behind). Untested fix: give probe-unseen candidates the best seen z, not 0.
3. Lexical wins retrieval and truth: only LSA-300 (0.702) beats TF-IDF char recall@10 0.666; no model reaches AUC 0.633 (best 0.614); every arena_best (0.122-0.288) is far under 0.687. LSA-300 is the strongest non-neural baseline (best kNN).

**Failures.** embeddinggemma-300m: gated (401, no token); logged and skipped. nomic: first run failed (transformers 5.21 dropped `get_extended_attention_mask`, which its remote code calls); rerun with a short shim, padding invariance checked (cosine 1.000000), remote code grepped first (no network, exec or env access). My bugs, fixed and re-run: `max_seq_length=inf` crash on static models; GloVe disk double-count (964 to 485 MB). Checks: the probe-recording subclass reproduces the unpatched harness (potion-8M probe all/1-4 0.836/0.819); hybrid equals zero-shot at probe weight 0, and the probe at zero-shot weight 0 (veto variant, rows with a seen candidate).
